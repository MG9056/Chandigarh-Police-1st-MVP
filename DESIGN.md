# Design Instructions

- Since this is a platform that will be used by police officers, it should be professional and clean and not whimisical or bloated.
- The site should provide both a light and dark mode using a toggle.
- The site should provide an option to change the language between English, Hindi and Punjabi. This should actually work in the site and not just be a dummy feature.
- The colors should be such that they are not straining on the eyes, and should be appropiate for police officers to use for long hours.
- The layout of the site should be such that it is easy to navigate and use. It should not be cluttered or confusing. Use three panels, the left which shows the general information, when u click on something inthe left it shoudl show the details in the mid panel and further details in the right panel.
- Use @references/light-theme for the light theme design as a reference but u will need to professionalize it agressively, and you can use it to inspire the dark theme design as well.
- Use green,yellow,red colors to display the severity of alerts or information in the platform
- For the dark theme you can take insipiration from @references/cdg-logo which has the chandigarh police logo
