# Project Overview
'Kala Jaddu' is a platform for detection of Illicit Drug sales on the dark net and other encrypted platfroms. This is a project built specifically for the Chandigarh Police to track illict drug sales on the darknet and other encrypted platforms. Use this intelligence to provide actionable intelligence to identify networks and lead to arrests.
